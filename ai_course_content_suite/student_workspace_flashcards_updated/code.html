Concept

Self-Attention Mechanism

How does the transformer determine the importance of different words in a sequence relative to a specific word?

Definition

It computes three vectors for each word: Query (Q), Key (K), and Value (V).

The attention score is calculated by taking the dot product of the Query with all Keys, scaling it, applying a softmax function to get weights, and multiplying these weights by the Values.
