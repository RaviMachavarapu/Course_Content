The artificial neuron

  • Weighted sum of inputs plus a bias
  • Passed through an activation function
  • Weights are learned from data

Activation functions

  • Sigmoid squashes to (0, 1)
  • ReLU is max(0, x) and trains fast
  • Non-linearity lets networks model curves

Layers and depth

  • Neurons are grouped into layers
  • Hidden layers build intermediate features
  • Deeper networks compose simpler features

Key takeaways

  • A network is many simple units combined
  • Training adjusts weights to reduce error
  • Depth and non-linearity give expressive power
